\section{Organization of This Thesis}

The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows.

\Cref{chap:background} reviews the foundations of quasi-Newton methods.
In \cref{sec:background_quasi_newton}, we briefly explain what quasi-Newton methods are.
In \cref{sec:background_modified_secant}, we explain the modified secant condition, which is used in our numerical experiments.
Since both topics are standard and well documented in the literature, we present them as a separate chapter.

\Cref{chap:main} presents the proposed regularized quasi-Newton method along with its theoretical analysis.
In \cref{sec:preliminaries}, we introduce the problem setting and review relevant background.
In \cref{sec:algorithm}, we present the proposed algorithm.
In \cref{sec:analysis}, we establish a global convergence property of the proposed algorithm.
In \cref{sec:proposed_choice}, we discuss practical choices of algorithmic variables and parameters.
In \cref{sec:experiments}, we report numerical results on the CUTEst benchmark collection.
Finally, in \cref{sec:conclusion}, we conclude the thesis with a summary and future research directions.
