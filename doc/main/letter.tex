  \opening{Dear Editors,}

  We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled ``Practical Regularized Quasi-Newton Methods with Inexact Function Values'' for publication in Mathematical Programming Computation as a Full Length Paper.

  This paper considers smooth nonconvex optimization problems in which function evaluations are affected by numerical errors.
  The main contributions of the paper are as follows:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item We develop a regularized quasi-Newton framework for smooth nonconvex optimization with noisy objective function evaluations.
    \item By combining a relaxed Armijo-type line search with a regularization parameter update inspired by Objective-Function-Free Optimization and AdaGrad-Norm, we establish a global convergence rate of $\mathcal{O}(1/\varepsilon^2)$ for obtaining a first-order stationary point.
    \item Numerical experiments on the CUTEst benchmark set with injected noise and low-precision floating-point arithmetic (64-, 32-, and 16-bit) demonstrate that the proposed method is substantially more robust and faster than several existing methods.
  \end{itemize}

  We believe that this work will be of interest to researchers in numerical optimization, particularly those studying quasi-Newton methods and optimization algorithms under noise.

  We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to Mathematical Programming Computation.

  Thank you for your consideration of our work.
